|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A student's age | int | Age is a whole number, so an int is the best choice. |
| 2 | The price of a coffee | double | A price can include a decimal, such as 3.50. |

| 4 | A student's middle initial | char | A char stores one character, such as 'J'. |
| 5 | A phone number | String | A phone number is used for contact, not math, and may contain symbols or leading zeros. |
| 6 | The population of New York City | int | An int can store whole numbers up to about 2.1 billion, which is enough for NYC's population. |
| 7 | A test score out of 100 | int | A test score is usually a whole number, such as 95. |
| 8 | A GPA | double | A GPA can have decimal places, such as 3.7. |
| 9 | Whether it is currently raining | boolean | A boolean can store true if it is raining or false if it is not. |
